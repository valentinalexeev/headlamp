Each person or owner is one row: open it for its devices and invitations. Actions follow the device's state: a link only before first use, Delete only after revoke. Traffic is sampled every 5 minutes and is not a connection log.

Invite a person

An invitation lets one person add one device. The person signs in with the identity provider; the device is made only when the verified email of the account is the invitation's. The person then picks the app. The invitation shows under the person above, or under "Invited, not signed in yet".

Tailnet

The tailnet targets of each device on each bridge in grants mode, from the device and from its owner. An owner grant applies to all devices of the owner, also to devices enrolled later.
